## Лор

- Заголовок: Сова летит туда, где ярче / The owl goes where the light is brightest
- Подводка: Ullu значит «сова». На хинди ещё и «дурак». Он и то и другое, и в деньгах никогда не ошибается. / Ullu means owl. In Hindi it also means a fool. He is both, and he is never wrong about money.
- 1. Он летает ночью. Каждую ночь Ullu летит над городом с мешком удачи. Имён на дверях он не читает. Он ищет самое яркое окно. / He flies at night. Every night Ullu flies over the city with a sack of fortune. He does not read names on doors. He looks for the brightest window.
- 2. Поэтому все зажигают огни. Лампы на подоконниках, огни на крышах, цветы на дверях. Каждый дом хочет, чтобы сова увидела его первым. / So everyone lights up. Lamps on the sills, lights on the roofs, flowers on the doors. Every house wants to be the one the owl sees first.
- 3. Семь ночей подряд. Зажигай дом каждую ночь. На седьмую ночь подряд сова садится на твою крышу, надевает тёмные очки и оставляет весь мешок. / Seven nights in a row. Light your house every night. On the seventh night in a row the owl lands on your roof, puts on his sunglasses and leaves the whole sack.

Правила семи ночей:
1. При первом заходе улица строит тебе дом. Он твой: такого больше ни у кого нет. / On your first visit the street builds you a house. It is yours: nobody else has the same one.
2. Раз в ночь жми «Зажечь». Сова пролетает и оставляет что-то: лампу, гирлянду, цветы, иногда один тапок. / Once a night, press Light up. The owl flies by and leaves something: a lamp, lights, flowers, sometimes one slipper.
3. Вешай подарки на дом. Каждую ночь дом ярче, а сова ближе. / Hang what he left on your house. Every night the house is brighter and the owl comes closer.
4. Пропустил ночь — дом гаснет. Счёт начинается с первой ночи. / Miss a night and the house goes dark. The run starts again from night one.
5. Седьмая ночь: сова садится на крышу с мешком золота. Сохрани карточку, поделись, выставь дом на улицу. / Night seven: the owl lands on your roof with a sack of gold. Save the card, share it, put the house on the street.

## Step 3: the seven nights and the night events (English draft, 05.11.2026)

Shown under the night counter, in the gift window and in the street diary. Words for every event live in `project.config.json` → `house.events`; an event without words never happens.

### The seven nights

1. Night one. The street is dark and quiet. One lamp in your window. Somewhere above, wings.
2. Night two. A stray cat moved onto your doorstep. Cats always know where the warm light is.
3. Night three. A chai cart parked next to your house. The street is starting to gather.
4. Night four. The neighbours hung lights of their own. It is a competition now.
5. Night five. Fireworks far away. A kid with a sparkler runs past your door.
6. Night six. Faces in every window. The whole street is watching the sky. So is the owl.
7. Night seven. The street goes quiet. The owl lands on your roof, puts on his sunglasses and opens the sack.

After night seven (in turn):

- Night {n}. The owl lives here now. He has opinions about your curtains.
- Night {n}. The owl invited his cousins. They are sitting on the wires.
- Night {n}. The street calls your house 'the owl house' now.
- Night {n}. The owl says the sack is getting lighter. He is lying.
- Night {n}. Your house is on the owl's map now. Marked in gold.
- Night {n}. The cat and the owl have agreed to share the roof. For now.

- Missed a night: Your house went dark. The cat left, the chai cart moved on, and the owl flew past without stopping. Light up tonight and start again.
- Not lit yet tonight: Light up and see what happens on your street tonight.
- Before the first night: Every night something happens on the street. Light the first lamp to see what.
